    My 1-min Pitch

    1.  Do you ever feel that you get bored very quickly when chating/interacting with ChatGPT, like talking to a person with no soul . A distinguish fact that separate human with machines are the ability to think with memories.Reasoning and memory has been long be the unique capabilities of human being, but can machines learn to do It. 
2. Our research aim to teach machine to perceive, think and memorize. We aim to train LLM and VLM’s to perceive long videos, understand what happens,  and memorize about entities and events. With that we aim to build a human-like episodic memory that enable the model to associate, retrieve and reason about the context when interacting with the outside world.  
3. Such research enables us to build long-term memory for LLM/VLM agents, that opens many new possibilities in the field of human-computer interaction
